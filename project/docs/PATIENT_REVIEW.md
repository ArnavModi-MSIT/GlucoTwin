# Patient review workflow

The dashboard supports research review using available history. These features do not change trained models, prediction horizons or reported performance.

- **Hourly history explorer:** a date-by-hour heatmap of the last seven study dates. Cell colour describes the observed hourly mean below 70, within 70–180 or above 180 mg/dL, rather than every individual reading. Displayed means and colour require a complete past hour with at least 10/12 observed readings. Supplied partial or poorly covered hours remain grey and can be opened for counts, coverage, mean/minimum/maximum, band fractions and checkpoint times. Future and unsupplied hours have no selectable observations. No gaps are filled.
- **Observed day comparison:** select two supplied dates to compare complete covered hours. Statistics use only hours available in both dates, weighting each matched hour equally. Lines show each date's available covered history, breaking across gaps; they describe past readings, not forecast trajectories or treatment effects. The default worked example selects two complete fictional days. A single supplied date or identical selections cannot produce a comparison.
- **Hourly history export:** Download hourly CSV saves the selected patient's available summaries, replay time and data cutoff through the tab's existing session. Partial/missing-hour counts remain explicit. It contains no forecasts, individual readings or care annotations. JSON snapshots also include the hourly rows; the existing printable reports do not include this heatmap or two-day chart.

- **Forecast inspection:** filter the numerical ledger by horizon and observed, pending, withheld or missing-target status. Newest/oldest ordering affects the table only. Counts state how many attempts are displayed; exports, pooled statistics and horizon cards still use the complete available session. Each horizon card compares the model and persistence using the same observed target pairs and reports the 90th percentile of absolute model errors (linear interpolation). Pending, withheld and missing targets do not enter those statistics. These overlapping replay rows are not independent validation samples. CGMacros offers its supported 60-minute horizon only.
- **Sensor history availability:** inspect 73 five-minute checkpoints over the visible six-hour window, including both endpoints, plus the last-hour count. Teal indicates a finite reading; grey indicates unavailable data. Runs distinguish time before the supplied chart history from missing checkpoints inside it. Represented time is slot count times five minutes, rather than a measured outage duration. Leading/trailing windows may be partial. The last observed checkpoint is shown separately and never substitutes for a missing current reading. CGMacros values are reconstructed source samples, not original sensor uptime. This view neither fills gaps nor changes admission rules.
- **Task inbox:** one patient-specific view of prescribed reminders and follow-up tasks, sorted due first, then upcoming, then completed/reviewed, each by study time. Filter open, due, upcoming, completed/reviewed or all available tasks. Only entries created by replay time are visible. Due entries can be marked reviewed/completed, edited or removed with the existing undo controls. Rewinding hides later completion states. Reviewing a reminder does not confirm medication was taken, and the inbox supplies no new prescribed times, notifications or treatment advice.

- **Patient at a glance:** observed 30-minute change requires all seven five-minute readings ending at the data cutoff. Seven-date band percentages are weighted by observed sample counts, with observed/expected coverage shown separately. Missing readings remain missing; reference bands are not personal care targets. Due and upcoming counts cover user-entered prescribed reminders and follow-up tasks, excluding future-created or already completed entries. Editing, completion and replay rewind update the counts.
- **Simulated sensor feed:** choose this scenario under Synthetic demo. It starts with six hours of past fictional readings. Receive next sample generates one new value; Simulate missing sample creates a missing slot. Start simulated feed generates arrivals at the selected demonstration speed; batch buttons receive 1, 6 or 12 slots. Each slot advances five study minutes and applies the five-minute availability delay. No future values are stored, and issued estimates remain fixed until the corresponding observation arrives. Missing slots withhold the trend rule until the entire last-hour window recovers. The session is bounded to 288 new slots (24 hours), can be reset and uses no real device, trained model or benchmark evidence.
- **Review report preview:** Preview review report pauses playback and captures the current patient snapshot and available notes in a modal. The preview includes the observed timeline with issued estimate markers and a covered past hourly median/percentile chart. Missing periods are gaps; future target observations are omitted. The preview stays fixed, and Download this report saves that same capture as standalone HTML for Print / Save as PDF. Closing or changing patients clears the preview. All text is escaped; a sandboxed frame and restrictive report policy prevent scripts, network resources and forms. No notes or report contents are uploaded.

- **Section navigation:** sticky links jump to Overview, Timeline, History, Care plan and Results. Overview opens its disclosure; keyboard focus moves to the destination. Unavailable sections cannot be selected.
- **Entry editing:** Edit opens the existing form for one entry. Save keeps the entry ID, validates its time/type and makes the revised content available at the current replay time. Prior completion state and captured meal results are cleared to avoid attaching old results to changed notes. Daily copies are edited independently. Prescribed reminders require confirmation again; Cancel edit leaves the entry unchanged.
- **Undo removal:** restores the latest removed entry for this patient, including its captured meal review. Up to ten recent removals are retained in tab memory. Restored IDs are reassigned when necessary to preserve newer entries. Undo respects the 200-entry limit, is lost on refresh and is cleared by Clear this patient's notes.
- **Refresh resume:** opt into Keep replay after refresh to retain an opaque session token, patient identity and replay time in this tab's session storage. The server's existing session supplies the exact clock and fixed ledgers; playback resumes paused. No sensor records, CSV contents or model artifacts are saved in this bookmark. One-hour inactivity expiry or a server restart starts a fresh available patient, with a visible notice; expired imported records need reimporting. Notes still require their separate local-saving option or JSON export. Turning resume off removes the bookmark. Browsers supporting Web Locks prevent a copied bookmark from sharing a replay with another active tab; a separate replay is started instead.

- **Patient overview:** lists available replay patients and their last viewed snapshot in the current browser tab. Unopened patients have no readings. Different issue times are explicit; this is not simultaneous live monitoring. Search and Open replay navigate the selected patient.
- **Forecast explanation:** Ridge forecasts show the latest available glucose, model reference offset and grouped contributions from glucose history, clinical profile, heart rate and missing-input indicators. Their sum reconstructs the forecast. Missing inputs use the fitted training median. Correlated/scaled feature contributions are arithmetic explanations, not causal effects or suggested interventions. Synthetic rules and withheld predictions have no Ridge explanation.
- **Fourteen-date history:** coverage, mean, standard deviation, coefficient of variation and overnight summaries use observed five-minute readings only. Complete dates need 288 slots and at least 70% coverage. Hourly percentile bands require three complete covered hours from different days, with at least 10/12 readings per hour. Percentiles describe past distributions, not future uncertainty. Missing periods remain gaps. This is a descriptive profile rather than a validated clinical AGP report.
- **Local care saving:** enable the patient-specific checkbox to retain notes and captured completed meal reviews across reloads. Local storage is unencrypted and specific to the browser/origin, including localhost port. Notes stay local and do not enter model training/inference. Storage failures and concurrent-tab changes are reported; export notes before reloading to preserve unsaved changes. Clear this patient's notes removes that patient's memory/local copy. Other patients are unaffected.
- **Retained meal reviews:** a completed observed window is captured only when its readings are available. A captured result can be displayed after the entire window leaves the six-hour chart. Rewinding before the capture cutoff hides it and computes from currently available readings instead. Removing the meal also removes its retained review. Captured results are locally stored descriptions, not verified dataset events. Ordinary JSON note exports transfer notes; retained windows are included in review summaries, not restored from the ordinary notes export.

HTML review reports include the extended history and model arithmetic. JSON snapshots include these fields; CSV retains forecast bounds and ledger values. User notes are included only in the separate browser-generated combined review summary. No automated medication timing, treatment recommendations, emergency messaging or device integration is provided.

Review observations and questions are dated no later than replay time. Follow-up tasks may be scheduled ahead and marked completed only when due; completion is hidden when replay is rewound. They are user-entered notes, not authenticated clinician findings or automatic advice. These entries remain separate from meal/medication timeline markers. Local saving and export/restore cover them using JSON notes schema v3, while v1/v2 files remain supported.
