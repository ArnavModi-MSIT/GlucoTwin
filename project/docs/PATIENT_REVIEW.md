# Patient review workflow

The dashboard supports research review using available history. These features do not change trained models, prediction horizons or reported performance.

- **Patient overview:** lists available replay patients and their last viewed snapshot in the current browser tab. Unopened patients have no readings. Different issue times are explicit; this is not simultaneous live monitoring. Search and Open replay navigate the selected patient.
- **Forecast explanation:** Ridge forecasts show the latest available glucose, model reference offset and grouped contributions from glucose history, clinical profile, heart rate and missing-input indicators. Their sum reconstructs the forecast. Missing inputs use the fitted training median. Correlated/scaled feature contributions are arithmetic explanations, not causal effects or suggested interventions. Synthetic rules and withheld predictions have no Ridge explanation.
- **Fourteen-date history:** coverage, mean, standard deviation, coefficient of variation and overnight summaries use observed five-minute readings only. Complete dates need 288 slots and at least 70% coverage. Hourly percentile bands require three complete covered hours from different days, with at least 10/12 readings per hour. Percentiles describe past distributions, not future uncertainty. Missing periods remain gaps. This is a descriptive profile rather than a validated clinical AGP report.
- **Local care saving:** enable the patient-specific checkbox to retain notes and captured completed meal reviews across reloads. Local storage is unencrypted and specific to the browser/origin, including localhost port. Notes stay local and do not enter model training/inference. Storage failures and concurrent-tab changes are reported; export notes before reloading to preserve unsaved changes. Clear this patient's notes removes that patient's memory/local copy. Other patients are unaffected.
- **Retained meal reviews:** a completed observed window is captured only when its readings are available. A captured result can be displayed after the entire window leaves the six-hour chart. Rewinding before the capture cutoff hides it and computes from currently available readings instead. Removing the meal also removes its retained review. Captured results are locally stored descriptions, not verified dataset events. Ordinary JSON note exports transfer notes; retained windows are included in review summaries, not restored from the ordinary notes export.

HTML review reports include the extended history and model arithmetic. JSON snapshots include these fields; CSV retains forecast bounds and ledger values. User notes are included only in the separate browser-generated combined review summary. No automated medication timing, treatment recommendations, emergency messaging or device integration is provided.
